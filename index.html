What this simulation assumes

This is a teaching model, not an eye prescription calculator. It treats the eye as a thin lens with a fixed retina, assumes glasses sit directly against the eye's optical system, and uses paraxial rays. Real eyes have separated optical surfaces, accommodation, pupil size, aberrations and individual anatomy.

A longer eyeball is one common cause of myopia, but the slider represents its optical effect as excess eye power—not literal eye growth.

Tip: choose “Myopia eye”, then press “Apply exact correction”. The glasses should become concave (negative power), and the focus should move back onto the retina.
